### TFP agreement

This is a trade-for-portfolio (TFP) collaboration. The Photographer and the Model agree to take part in the photo session described in this contract. No fee is payable by either party.

The Photographer will deliver the agreed selection of edited images to the Model for the Model's personal portfolio and self-promotion. The number of images, the file format and the delivery date will be agreed before the session.

The Photographer owns the copyright in the images and grants the Model a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the delivered images in the Model's personal portfolio, website and social media. The Model must credit the Photographer where the platform allows it and may not sell, sublicense or use the images in advertising or commercial campaigns without the Photographer's written permission.

The Model grants the Photographer a non-exclusive, worldwide license to use the images in the Photographer's portfolio, website, social media and marketing, with credit to the Model where the platform allows it. The Photographer may use the images to promote their photography business, but may not advertise a third-party product or service with them, or sell or license them to a third party, without the Model's written permission.

The Model confirms that they are at least 18 years old, or that a parent or legal guardian signs this agreement on their behalf. Where data protection law applies, the Model consents to the Photographer processing their personal data, including their image, for the purposes of this agreement and the uses described above. Neither party will use the images in a defamatory, pornographic or otherwise unlawful manner.

If either party cannot attend the session, they will give the other as much notice as possible so it can be rescheduled. A party who fails to attend without notice may have to cover the other party's reasonable costs and may be excluded from future collaborations.

This agreement is governed by the laws of the country where the photo session takes place.
