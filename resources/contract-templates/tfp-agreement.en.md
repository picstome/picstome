### TFP agreement

This is a trade-for-portfolio (TFP) collaboration. The Photographer and the Model agree to take part in the photo session described in this contract without monetary compensation.

The Photographer will deliver a selection of edited images to the Model for the Model's personal portfolio and self-promotion, as agreed before the session.

The Model grants the Photographer the right to use the images in the Photographer's portfolio, website, social media and marketing. The Photographer grants the Model the right to use the delivered images in the Model's personal portfolio and self-promotion, with credit to the Photographer where the platform allows it.

Neither party may sell or license the images to a third party without the written permission of the other. The Photographer owns the copyright in the images.

If either party cannot attend the session, they will give the other as much notice as possible so the session can be rescheduled.
